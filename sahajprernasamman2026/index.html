About

About the event

Short, clear context for attendees and invitees.

Sahaj Prerna Samman 2026 recognises inspiring women contributors whose work reflects service, leadership, compassion and social responsibility.

The event is dedicated to the values of Lokmata Ahilyabai Holkar — justice, public welfare, cultural preservation and inclusive leadership.

Sahaj Kalatmak Foundation

A cultural and social organisation working through art, literature, theatre, awareness and value-based public engagement.
