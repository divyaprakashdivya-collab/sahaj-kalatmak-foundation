An Evening of Inspiration & Recognition

Sahaj Prerna Samman 2026 celebrates inspiring women whose remarkable journeys reflect service, leadership, compassion, dignity, resilience, and social responsibility — honouring purpose-driven contributions that create lasting impact, inspire communities, and strengthen the spirit of a progressive Bharat.
